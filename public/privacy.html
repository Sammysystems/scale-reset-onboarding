Privacy

What I collect, and why.

Last updated 11 November 2026

What you share

The check asks about your business, the work that slips, the tools you use, and how to reach you. If you book a time, your details are saved here and you continue the conversation on WhatsApp. If a build starts, I collect what is needed to deliver it, such as account access, process notes, and the details of the work.

Why I use it

To answer your enquiry, prepare the call, write a scope and price, deliver the build, and keep the records needed to run it. I do not sell your information, and I do not use it to send marketing you did not ask for.

Where it lives

Check answers and booking details are stored in the app's database and can be seen by the operator. The conversation continues on WhatsApp under its own terms. Build work may use the tools you already use, with access limited to what the build needs. Some providers process data outside Nigeria.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not become work are kept for up to 12 months, then removed. Build records are kept for as long as the system runs, or as long as the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be removed. You can revoke access to any tool at any time. Use the contact details on your scope or booking confirmation to make a request.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to Samuel through the same channel that sent your scope or booking confirmation.
